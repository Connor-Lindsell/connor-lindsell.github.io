## The Role

Working as an undergraduate mechanical and mechatronic engineer within Capral's
manufacturing operations, gaining exposure to aluminium extrusion processes,
production engineering, and applied manufacturing problem-solving in an
industrial setting.

- Data/simulation driven mechanical design for new projects, existing infrastructure upgrades and maintenance projects. Applying DFM and DFA design intent to ensure projects are created efficiently, within budget and functional for the given task.
- Exposure to aluminium extrusion and manufacturing engineering workflows
- Cross-functional collaboration with multidisciplinary teams, both technical and non-technical
- Applying engineering fundamentals to real industrial processes
